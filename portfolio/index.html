Case 01

A checkout that looked broken

JD Sports Group (Sprinter and Sportzone) · Senior Digital & Marketing Data Analyst · 2021–2024

The business wanted the checkout redesigned. Before anyone committed engineering time, I compared the funnel step by step with the retail benchmark, and found a page carrying of checkout traffic that had never been tracked. I fixed that first.

Three teams wanted three different fixes. Marketing wanted Customer Care on video calls, Customer Care wanted chat on product pages, and the evidence pointed at the checkout. I showed each team, step by step, what data existed at each stage and what each option would tell us, and we started with the checkout.

Then we went after the reasons, with a Hotjar survey categorised through the OpenAI API and live chat reasons logged by Customer Care in Salesforce. Payment failures came out as the main cause. After the changes shipped, checkout conversion rose against the pre-launch baseline, and Customer Care kept using the weekly report in its own meetings.

Case 02

One set of numbers for everyone

JD Sports Group · product owner of an 8-project measurement programme · 2021–2024

Marketing and ecommerce didn't trust each other's figures. With the director, I started from a measurement plan and agreed objectives with each area before defining any KPI. I ordered the backlog by dependency and by how many teams each piece unblocked, so no one built a metric that hadn't been agreed. Each delivery came with a working dashboard and training.

New analytics requests grew year on year, with stakeholder satisfaction.

Case 05

Keeping a person in the loop

Nutr(IA) Coach · co-founder and AI product manager · 2024 to now, in beta

Nutrition professionals had no AI tool built around their client work. I took the product from concept to beta. I coordinate the team and decide what to build next from interviews with more than ten of them.

We chose to give the AI to nutritionists only, so a professional reviews each recommendation before it reaches a client, and I run evals on the LLM features. The project reached the final phase of the INCOOVA incubator, ventures.
